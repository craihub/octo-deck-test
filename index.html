Getting the test version

  1. Activate your key

    The easiest way is in a web browser: go to store.steampowered.com/account/registerkey, sign in and paste the key. You can also do it in the Steam app with Games → Activate a Product on Steam…

  2. Switch to the test build

    On the Deck, open the game in your Library, press the ⚙ gear → Properties → Betas and choose rctest. No password is needed. Let it update. Without this step you get an older build.

  3. Play normally

    Use the Deck's controls; you don't need a keyboard. A few full dives is perfect. If something goes wrong, take a screenshot (STEAM + R1) so you can send it too.

Deck buttons

In a fight, B or L2 spins on the spot. When the room is safe, hold it to zoom, and zoom at a hole or a small rock to hop right over it. The touch screen works too: drag on the left half to swim and the right half to aim.

What to check

Did it start and play fine?
Controls Swimming, aiming, spinning, mines, items, menus and the pause menu, using the buttons above. Did the prompts on screen show Deck buttons rather than keyboard keys?
Zooming and hopping When a room is safe, hold B or L2 to zoom. Zoom at a hole or a small rock to hop it. In a fight, B or L2 spins on the spot.
Cute stuff (stand still for a while when it's safe)
Reading text on the Deck screen Tip: Settings → TEXT SIZE makes everything bigger.
How smooth was it? Especially on later floors with lots of foes and effects. The Deck's own performance overlay (… button → battery icon) shows frames per second if you want a number.
Battery and heat Settings → FRAME RATE can cap the game (cooler, quieter, longer battery). Try it capped and uncapped if you can.
Sleep and the Steam button During a dive, press the power button to sleep, wake it up, and keep playing. Also press the STEAM button mid-fight: does the game pause?
